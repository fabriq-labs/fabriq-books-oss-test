# Fabriq Books — User Onboarding Guide

> Step-by-step sessions for finance teams, bookkeepers, and accountants learning Fabriq Books.  
> Each session explains **how Fabriq works** in depth, using **screen names and field labels as they appear in the app** (sidebar menus, form titles, column headers). **Zoho Books help links** appear at the end of every session for users migrating from or comparing with Zoho.

**Last updated:** June 2026

---

## Overview

This guide walks you through Fabriq Books in **seven sessions**, from first login to month-end reporting. Work through them in order if you are new; otherwise jump to the session that matches your daily work.

| Session | Guide | Duration | What you will learn |
|---------|-------|----------|---------------------|
| 1 | [Overview & Getting Started](./01-overview/README.md) | ~30 min | What Fabriq does, sign-in, entity selector, list-page basics |
| 2 | [Common Features](./02-common-features/README.md) | ~45 min | Filters, import, CSV mapper, bulk edit, bulk delete |
| 3 | [Sales — Order to Cash](./03-sales/README.md) | ~60 min | Customers, quotes, orders, invoices, receipts, credit notes |
| 4 | [Purchases & Workflows](./04-purchases/README.md) | ~75 min | Suppliers, PR, PO, bills — full approval workflows |
| 5 | [Accounting & Banking](./05-accounting/README.md) | ~60 min | Chart of accounts, transactions, bank feeds, opening balances |
| 6 | [Reports & Month-End](./06-reports/README.md) | ~45 min | Balance sheet, P&L, trial balance, cash flow, Posting List |
| 7 | [Dashboard, Masters & Settings](./07-other-menus/README.md) | ~30 min | Dashboard, Workbench, investments, masters, settings |

## Recommended paths

### New user (first week)

1. [Session 1 — Overview](./01-overview/README.md)  
2. [Session 2 — Common Features](./02-common-features/README.md) — learn filters and import once; you will use these everywhere  
3. Your daily module — [Sales](./03-sales/README.md), [Purchases](./04-purchases/README.md), or [Accounting](./05-accounting/README.md)

### Month-end close

1. [Session 5 — Accounting → Transactions](./05-accounting/README.md) — categorize and reconcile  
2. [Session 6 — Reports](./06-reports/README.md) — trial balance, P&L, balance sheet

### Migrating from Zoho Books

1. [Session 2 — Import](./02-common-features/README.md) — understand Fabriq’s CSV Mapper (different from Zoho’s single-page wizard)  
2. Export data from Zoho — see the **Zoho Books reference** at the end of Session 2  
3. Import masters first (customers, suppliers, items, accounts), then transactions — Sessions 3 and 4

## Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Transaction (journal entry)** | A ledger line with a **Debit Account**, **Credit Account**, and **Amount** — what you see on **Accounting → Transactions** |
| **Chart of Accounts** | The list of accounts under **Accounting → Chart of Accounts** |
| **Purchase Requisition (PR)** | Internal buy request under **Purchases → Purchase Requisitions** |
| **Purchase Order (PO)** | Confirmed order to a supplier under **Purchases → Purchase Orders** |
| **Accounts Receivable (AR)** | Money customers owe you — tracked on sales invoices and payment receipts |
| **Accounts Payable (AP)** | Money you owe suppliers — tracked on bills |
| **Linked field** | A form field that picks a record from another list (e.g. **Customer** on an invoice, **Item** on a line) |
| **Workflow status** | Where a document sits in approval — shown on the document and in workflow tabs (e.g. Draft, Manager Review) |
| **Legal entity** | The company whose books you are viewing — selected in the top bar |
