## Slide 2: The problem

**Finance teams are stuck between spreadsheets and point solutions**

- Reconciling bank and ledger is manual and error-prone
- AR: chasing overdue invoices and matching payments is repetitive
- Data lives in QuickBooks, Xero, Zoho — and in PDFs and spreadsheets
- “Agentic” tools are either enterprise (HighRadius) or narrow (single-ERP add-ons)

**Speaker note**: Set up “we’re different”: one layer on top of what you already use.

---

## Slide 3: Our approach

**One agent. Your ERPs. Your bank data.**

- **Plug in** to QuickBooks, Xero, Zoho (and import bank/CC statements)
- **One place** for transactions, invoices, payments — normalized
- **Agent** answers: what’s overdue? what’s unreconciled? who to chase? suggest match
- **No rip-and-replace**: we sit on top of your existing accounting system

**Speaker note**: No cards, no new GL — we add intelligence and workflow.

---

## Slide 4: Two stories we’re leading with

**1. Bank / ledger reconciliation**

- “Which bank transactions are unreconciled?”
- “What does this transaction match?” → suggest payment or invoice
- “Explain this transaction” → one-line explanation
- **Audience**: Controllers, accountants, firms doing recs for clients

**2. Accounts receivable**

- “What’s overdue?” “What’s our DSO?” “Who should I follow up with?”
- “Suggest which invoices this payment applies to” → apply with one click
- **Audience**: AR managers, CFOs, bookkeepers offering AR services

## Slide 12: Why Fabriq

**Same platform. Same agent. Reconciliation + AR.**

- **Multi-ERP**: One layer across QuickBooks, Xero, Zoho (NetSuite coming)
- **Bank data**: Import CSV/PDF today; live bank feed (e.g. Plaid) later
- **Agentic**: Ask questions and get actions (suggest match, apply payment), not just reports
- **Direct or partner**: Use it yourself or offer it to clients (accounting firms, bookkeepers)

**Speaker note**: Position as “finance operations layer” not “another AR tool.”

## Slide 13: Who it’s for

**Direct GTM**

- SMB / mid-market finance: controller, AR manager, CFO
- Already on QB, Xero, or Zoho; want better reconciliation and AR without swapping systems

**Partner GTM**

- Accounting firms doing reconciliation and AR for clients
- Bookkeepers and fractional CFOs offering “AR as a service” or “reconciliation as a service”

**Roadmap (after clean demo)**
