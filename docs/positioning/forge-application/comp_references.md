Agentic AI refers to autonomous AI systems that plan, execute multi-step tasks, and adapt using reasoning, tools, and learning, unlike traditional rule-based automation. In financial platforms like Ramp, Rillet, and Kick, it automates complex workflows such as invoice processing, fraud detection, and reconciliations to achieve faster closes and reduced manual work. [stack-ai](https://www.stack-ai.com/insights/how-ramp-s-agentic-ai-transforms-corporate-finance-automation-and-expense-management)

## Ramp
Ramp uses specialized agentic AI agents in its Bill Pay system for end-to-end accounts payable automation. These include auto-coding for GL assignments, fraud prevention scanning 60+ signals, approval agents providing context summaries, and payment optimization for card usage and discounts. The agents learn from historical data, handle exceptions autonomously, and integrate with ERPs for touchless processing, cutting invoice times significantly. [ramp](https://ramp.com/blog/agentic-ai/agentic-ai-for-accounts-payable)

## Rillet
Rillet is an AI-native ERP for mid-market finance teams, integrating with Ramp, Stripe, and others for zero-day closes. Its Aura AI and agent marketplace automate bank reconciliations, flux analysis, revenue recognition (ASC 606), accruals, and journal entries via hyper-specific agents. Features like natural language queries and multi-entity consolidation enable proactive workflows with minimal oversight. [everydev](https://www.everydev.ai/tools/rillet)

## Kick
Kick offers "self-driving bookkeeping" as an agentic system that proactively categorizes transactions, matches receipts, and prepares statements after setup. Backed by OpenAI fund, it runs in the background with minimal intervention, anticipating needs like predictive insights while keeping users in control. It represents a shift to autonomous financial agents for small businesses. [skywork](https://skywork.ai/skypage/en/Unlocking-Financial-Efficiency:-A-Deep-Dive-into-Kick-Bookkeeping-for-AI-Users/1976193790739410944)
